# Historical security assessment

Frozen pre-default assessment. Its proposals and open decisions describe the earlier design, not current policy. Current authority: [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md). Original 26 scenarios, 93 elements, 558 dispositions and source/review caveats are preserved below. Subsequent decisions were authorized by the user on 2026-10-13; they are not runtime verification.

## Threat Model

**ASSUMPTION:** Consider unauthenticated requesters, authenticated users exceeding their eventual permissions, malicious browser content, modified mobile clients, network attackers, and compromised dependencies or operational credentials. These are threat scenarios, not allegations about the listed actors. Deployment exposure, likelihood, impact ratings, and accepted residual risk are TO BE DECIDED. An otherwise authorized admin may also misuse granted operations; this is a capability scenario, not an allegation.

**ASSUMPTION:** Protect the integrity and permitted disclosure of the application-owned tournament, participant, match, plan, score, statistics, and ranking data. Passkey account associations, credential records, session material, configuration secrets, and security logs are additional security assets whose schema and storage are TO BE DECIDED. Commerce catalog/menu, stock, order/sale history, price snapshots and totals defined in [REQUIREMENTS.md](https://github.com/jmanico/aces-and-ale/blob/5f1aa04e24003b840ca1f2de397973f3bb90ac91/REQUIREMENTS.md) are additional integrity assets; commerce records are internal under SEC-ACCESS-02.

### Element and STRIDE Coverage

The following local element IDs index the cited owner evidence; they do not introduce components or deployment decisions. S = Spoofing, T = Tampering, R = Repudiation, I = Information Disclosure, D = Denial of Service, E = Elevation of Privilege. A T-ID denotes the linked scenario’s consequence on that element, not a claim that a data record executes code. **N** = assessed, no evidenced scenario within this element’s documented role; **A** = actor context, categories are assessed at the surfaces those actors use; **U** = not assessed because required scope/authority/mechanism evidence is missing. These dispositions describe analysis coverage, not implementation conformance.

U on ranking tampering/accountability depends on ranking source/edit authority (SQ-3/SQ-5). U on platform-facility internals depends on platform/adoption decisions (SQ-1/SQ-2). U on operational identity/availability depends on SQ-8/SQ-9/SQ-10. F-IP is a dotted mapping dependency, not an established runtime flow; all its categories remain U under SQ-2/SQ-8. The static public logo has only artifact-integrity exposure here. Public tournament/plan/score projections have no confidentiality threat merely because another public reference is read; protected associations/extra fields remain T-5. No deployed zones are inferred.
